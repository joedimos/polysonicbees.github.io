# Joseph Dimos — research & engineering portfolio

A responsive, accessible, zero-build public portfolio presenting research in applied mathematics, scientific computing, machine learning, and formal reasoning.

## Contents

- `index.html`: biography, research directions, open projects, documented scholarship, CV-based background, contact.
- `favicon.svg`: monogram favicon.
- `bio.html`: polished short and expanded public biography.
- `PUBLIC_BIO.md`: reusable professional biography and primary sources.

## Publishing on GitHub Pages

In this repository, go to **Settings → Pages** and select **Deploy from a branch**, then **main** and **/(root)**, and click Save.

The expected public URL after GitHub Pages is enabled is:
https://joedimos.github.io/polysonicbees.github.io/

## Editorial policy

- Career and education statements are based on the supplied CV and are not presented as independently confirmed employer or university records.
- The publication links to its DOI; seminar and research participation claims link to event/program records.
- Only public repositories are linked. Private software, client code, health, family, pay, home address, and personal records are not disclosed.
- No invented credentials, impact metrics, contributions to upstream projects, grants, or awards.
- Some work is clearly identified as exploratory or in development.

## Preview

Run `python -m http.server 8000` and open `http://localhost:8000`. No build dependencies.
