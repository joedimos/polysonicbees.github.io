01 / About

A connected research practice.

My work connects theoretical mathematics with scientific and computational problems that demand rigor, clarity, and executable results.

My interests span category theory, mathematical logic, dynamical systems, numerical methods for partial differential equations, and scientific machine learning. A recurring question across these areas is how to preserve meaningful structure when we move between models, resolutions, representations, and implementations.

In parallel, I develop practical software for data-intensive workflows, including automation, document understanding, structured reasoning, and technical decision support. These projects extend the same emphasis on explicit assumptions, reproducibility, and careful validation.

02 / Research

Lines of inquiry.

Theoretical foundations and computational methods are most useful when developed together. These four themes organize my current and prior work.

01 — Mathematics

Category theory & formal structure

Compositional descriptions of systems; chain complexes and topology; logical semantics; commutativity and naturality as testable properties of computational models.

02 — Physical computation

Geophysical flows & numerical methods

Finite-volume discretization, cut-cell meshes, irregular bathymetry, conservation, multiscale modeling, and numerical analysis for ocean and climate problems.

03 — Learning & dynamics

Scientific machine learning

Reduced-order models, Koopman and Mori–Zwanzig approaches, dynamical systems, uncertainty, and machine learning for physical processes.

04 — Logic & intelligence

Reasoning & verification

Argumentation frameworks, normative and temporal logic, proof-carrying computation, executable semantics, and correctness-oriented AI systems.

01Scientific computing · Research framework

cut_cell_cat

A research framework for finite-volume conservation and mathematical interfaces to categorical structures, stochastic kernels, and resolution changes. Its emphasis is on making conservation and cross-scale consistency claims precise—and distinguishing valid laws from properties that do not automatically hold.

02Ocean modeling · Prototype

OceanCutCells.jl

A Julia package exploring cut-cell geometry and cell merging for ocean simulations over irregular seafloor topography. The implementation connects geometry and finite-volume physics within the Oceananigans ecosystem, with example simulations and geometry tests.
